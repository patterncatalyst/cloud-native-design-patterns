The chapters and appendices draw on a small set of books on microservices, distributed data,
event-driven systems, API design, coupling and production stability. They are reference works,
not sources: no text or code from any of them is reproduced here. Where a topic in this site is
useful to you, these are the places to go deeper.

| Title | Author(s) | Publisher | Year | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [*Building Microservices*, 2nd ed.](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/building-microservices-2nd/9781492034018/) | Sam Newman | O'Reilly | 2021 | Service boundaries and integration |
| [*Monolith to Microservices*](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/monolith-to-microservices/9781492047834/) | Sam Newman | O'Reilly | 2019 | Incremental decomposition patterns such as the strangler fig (Appendix K) |
| [*Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, 2nd ed.](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-data-intensive-applications/9781098119058/) | Martin Kleppmann, Chris Riccomini | O'Reilly | 2026 | Data and consistency foundations behind the outbox and the saga |
| [*Building Event-Driven Microservices*, 2nd ed.](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/building-event-driven-microservices/9798341622180/) | Adam Bellemare | O'Reilly | 2025 | The event backbone: event streams, schemas and event-driven services |
| [*Cloud Native Patterns*](https://www.manning.com/books/cloud-native-patterns) | Cornelia Davis | Manning | 2019 | Change-tolerant cloud-native design |
| [*Patterns of Distributed Systems*](https://www.informit.com/store/patterns-of-distributed-systems-9780138221980) | Unmesh Joshi | Addison-Wesley | 2023 | Replication, consensus and partitioning building blocks |

| [*Cloud Native Anti-Patterns*](https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/product/cloud-native-anti-patterns-9781836200581) | Gerald Bachlmayr, Aiden Ziegelaar, Alan Blockley, Bojan Zivic | Packt | 2025 | Common cloud-native mistakes and how to refactor them |
| [*Principles of Web API Design*](https://www.informit.com/store/principles-of-web-api-design-delivering-value-with-9780137355631) | James Higginbotham | Addison-Wesley | 2021 | An outcome-driven API design process |
| [*API Design Patterns*](https://www.manning.com/books/api-design-patterns) | JJ Geewax | Manning | 2021 | Resource and operation design, versioning |
| [*Patterns for API Design*](https://www.informit.com/store/patterns-for-api-design-simplifying-integration-with-9780137670109) | Olaf Zimmermann, Mirko Stocker, Daniel Lübke, Uwe Zdun, Cesare Pautasso | Addison-Wesley | 2022 | Message-exchange, quality and evolution patterns for APIs |

| [*Balancing Coupling in Software Design*](https://www.informit.com/store/balancing-coupling-in-software-design-universal-design-9780137353538) | Vlad Khononov | Addison-Wesley | 2024 | Integration strength, distance and volatility (Appendix G) |
| [*Release It!*, 2nd ed.](https://pragprog.com/titles/mnee2/release-it-second-edition/) | Michael T. Nygard | Pragmatic Bookshelf | 2018 | Stability patterns: timeouts, circuit breakers, bulkheads (Appendix M) |
| [*Site Reliability Engineering*](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) | Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff, Niall Richard Murphy (eds.) | O'Reilly | 2016 | Cascading failures and handling overload; free to read online (Appendix M) |

For contracts, versioning and error formats, the relevant IETF RFCs and drafts sit alongside
these books; each chapter names the ones it relies on.

Every title here is also on the PatternCatalyst
[Recommended Reading](https://patterncatalyst.github.io/patterncatalyst-workshops-tutorials-list/books/)
list, which collects the books referenced across all of our workshops and tutorials.
